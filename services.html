What services do you offer?

Our services include physiotherapy, chiropractic, aesthetics, Botox, motor vehicle accident (MVA) care and extended health care.

Call +1 (416) 752-7434 or email canadianhealthproviders@gmail.com to discuss an appointment.

Do I need a referral?

Please check with the clinic before booking. Requirements may depend on the service and your insurance arrangements.

Can I ask about fees and insurance before booking?

Yes. Contact the clinic to confirm fees and billing arrangements. Check coverage requirements with your insurer where applicable.

What should I include in my online enquiry?

Include your name, preferred contact method, the service you are interested in and a brief appointment enquiry. Please avoid sending detailed medical information through the general enquiry form.

Can I request an appointment on the weekend?

The clinic is open seven days a week, 10:00 AM–7:00 PM. Contact us to confirm weekend appointment availability.

Does an online request confirm my appointment?

An online request starts the conversation. Our team will contact you to confirm availability and your appointment date and time.
