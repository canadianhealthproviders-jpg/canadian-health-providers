How do I request an appointment?

Use the online request form, email canadianhealthproviders@gmail.com or call +1 (416) 666-3486. The clinic will confirm appointment availability.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday through Sunday, from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Where is the clinic located?

Unit 1A07-7215 Goreway Dr, Mississauga, ON L4T 0B4.

Is an online request a confirmed booking?

No. An appointment request is subject to confirmation. Please wait for the clinic to confirm your appointment date and time.

How can I check services, fees or insurance details?

Please contact the clinic before your visit for current service information, fees, referral requirements and insurance or billing arrangements.
