Helpful information

Before you contact us.

A few details to help you plan your enquiry and your visit.

Is my appointment confirmed after submitting?

No. The clinic must confirm availability and your appointment date and time.

Can I contact the clinic without using the form?

Yes. Call +1 (416) 666-3486 or email canadianhealthproviders@gmail.com .

What information should I send?

Your name, preferred contact details and a brief enquiry are enough to start. Avoid sending detailed medical or identification information through this form.

When is the clinic open?

Monday through Sunday, from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
